# Certification

Certification is version-, executable-, node-, environment-, policy-, and connector-revision
bound. Fake executables can prove adapter conformance but can never prove real-binary support.

Profiles cover discovery, adapter behavior, authentication, isolated read-only work, separately
approved writes and commands, cancellation and process-tree cleanup, and session resume where
supported. Evidence records capability results and an evidence digest. A missing account,
subscription, or paid-inference approval is recorded as a validation gap, not converted into a
pass.
